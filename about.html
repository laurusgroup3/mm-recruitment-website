About MM Recruitment

A people consultancy, built on genuine connection.

We believe that behind every CV is a person with a story, and behind every vacancy is a team who needs the right addition - not just a name to fill a gap.

Our philosophy

People make a brighter tomorrow.

That's not just our strapline - it's the reason MM Recruitment exists. We started this business because we believe recruitment done well is one of the most meaningful things you can do: helping someone find work that suits them, and helping a business find someone who will genuinely thrive within it.

We're not interested in volume for its own sake. We're interested in getting it right - for the person, and for the business.
