1. About these terms

These terms apply when your organisation’s authorised users create and use accounts on the MM Recruitment employer portal at portal.mmrecruitmentltd.co.uk (“the portal”). They govern use of the portal only. Recruitment services, fees and placements are covered by your organisation’s separate written terms of business with MM Recruitment MM to confirm: that those terms of business take priority if the two conflict. By creating an account you agree to these terms on behalf of your organisation.

2. Who we are

MM Recruitment Ltd is a company registered in England and Wales, company number 16835419. Registered office: 32 Portway Drive, Croughton, Brackley, England, NN13 5NA.

3. Accounts and authorised users

  • New employer accounts are linked to your organisation by MM Recruitment before full access is given.
  • Only people your organisation has authorised may use its account. Your organisation is responsible for its users and for telling us promptly when someone should no longer have access.
  • Each user must verify their email address, keep their password secure and not share their login.

4. What the portal lets you do

  • maintain your company profile;
  • create and manage vacancies;
  • view candidates MM Recruitment has submitted to you, and give feedback on them;
  • view placements; and
  • receive updates and messages from MM Recruitment.

5. Candidate information

Candidate profiles and CVs are shared with you only for the vacancy they were submitted for. You must use them only to assess candidates for that vacancy, keep them confidential and secure, comply with UK data protection law, and not contact or engage a submitted candidate other than as your terms of business with MM Recruitment allow.

Vacancy details you add must be accurate and lawful, including compliance with equality and employment law. You allow MM Recruitment to use them to recruit for your vacancies.

8. Acceptable use

Do not misuse the portal, including trying to access candidate or other organisations’ data beyond what has been shared with you, interfering with its security, or using automated tools to extract information from it.
