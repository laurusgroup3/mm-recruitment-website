2. Our website and portal

This policy covers our website at mmrecruitmentltd.co.uk and our candidate and employer portal at portal.mmrecruitmentltd.co.uk. The portal runs on Laurus Operating System (LOS) technology, which processes personal information on our behalf and only on our instructions. MM to confirm: the LOS provider's legal entity name and that a data processing agreement is in place.

3. Information we collect

Enquiries. When you use our contact or employer enquiry forms: your name, email address, phone number (if given), company (for employer enquiries) and your message.

Candidate accounts. Your name, email address and password (handled by our secure sign-in service); contact details; profile headline and postcode area; skills, work experience, availability and job preferences; your CV and supporting documents; the roles you apply for and the employers your profile is shared with; messages between you and MM Recruitment; your marketing preference; and any organisations you ask us not to share your profile with.

Employer accounts. The names, work email addresses and roles of your authorised users; your company profile; your vacancies; your feedback on candidates we submit; placements; and messages between you and MM Recruitment.

Technical information. When you sign in to the portal, your sign-in session is kept in your browser (see our Cookie & Browser Storage Policy). Our hosting provider processes standard technical information, such as IP addresses, to deliver and protect the website and portal.

4. How we use your information

  • to respond to your enquiries;
  • to create and manage your portal account;
  • to provide recruitment services, including matching candidates with vacancies and, for candidates, sharing your profile and CV with prospective employers for specific roles;
  • to send you updates about your account, applications or vacancies, in the portal and by email;
  • to send marketing, only where you have agreed to it (you can withdraw this at any time in the portal);
  • to keep the portal secure, including keeping a record of important account and recruitment actions; and
  • to meet our legal obligations.

We do not sell your personal information.

5. Legal basis for processing

MM to confirm: the lawful basis under UK GDPR for each purpose in section 4 (for example contract, legitimate interests, consent for marketing, legal obligation).

6. How long we keep your information

MM to confirm: retention periods for enquiries, candidate accounts and CVs, employer accounts, and records kept after an account is closed. You can permanently delete your candidate account at any time from Account and privacy in the portal.

7. Sharing your information

Prospective employers. We share candidate profiles and CVs with employers for specific vacancies, and never with an organisation on a candidate’s exclusion list. Candidates can see in the portal who their information has been shared with.

Service providers acting on our behalf:

  • Laurus Operating System (LOS) – the portal’s recruitment technology;
  • Supabase – account sign-in, database and document storage for the portal;
  • Cloudflare – website and portal hosting and security;
  • Resend – delivery of email notifications and enquiry emails;
  • Google – web fonts used by the website and portal, and Google sign-in for MM Recruitment staff only.

MM to confirm: where these providers store and process data, and the safeguards used for any transfer outside the UK.

We may also share information where required by law.

8. Your rights

Under UK data protection law you have the right to access your information, to have it corrected or erased, to restrict or object to certain processing, to data portability, and to withdraw consent where we rely on it. Candidates can export their data and delete their account from Account and privacy in the portal. To exercise any right, contact us at mandy@mmrecruitmentltd.co.uk.

9. Complaints

If you’re unhappy with how we’ve handled your information, please contact us first. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk.

10. Contact us

For any privacy questions, contact mandy@mmrecruitmentltd.co.uk or use our contact page.
