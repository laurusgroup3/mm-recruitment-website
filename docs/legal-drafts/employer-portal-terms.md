## 4. What the portal lets you do

Through the portal your authorised users can:

- maintain your company profile;
- create and manage vacancies and their requirements;
- view candidates MM Recruitment has submitted to you, and give
  feedback on them;
- view placements; and
- receive updates and messages from MM Recruitment.

## 5. Candidate information

Candidate profiles and CVs are shared with you only for the specific
vacancy they were submitted for. You must:

- use candidate information only to assess candidates for that vacancy;
- keep it confidential and secure, and not pass it to anyone outside
  your organisation's hiring process;
- comply with UK data protection law (UK GDPR and the Data Protection
  Act 2018) when handling it; and
- not contact or engage a submitted candidate other than as set out in
  your terms of business with MM Recruitment. **[MM TO CONFIRM.]**
