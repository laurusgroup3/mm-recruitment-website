## 4. What the portal lets you do

Through your account you can:

- maintain your profile: contact details, headline, location area,
  skills, work experience, availability and job preferences;
- upload, replace and download your CV and supporting documents (PDF or
  Word, up to 5MB each);
- view open roles and track the applications and submissions made for
  you;
- receive updates and messages from MM Recruitment, and reply to them;
- see which employers your profile or CV has been shared with, and for
  which vacancy;
- list organisations you do not want your profile shared with;
- set marketing and notification preferences; and
- use your data rights from within the portal, including exporting your
  data and permanently deleting your account.
