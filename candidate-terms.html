1. About these terms

These terms apply when you create and use a candidate account on the MM Recruitment portal at portal.mmrecruitmentltd.co.uk (“the portal”). They sit alongside our Privacy Policy, which explains how we handle your personal information, and our Cookie & Browser Storage Policy. By creating an account you agree to these terms.

2. Who we are

MM Recruitment Ltd is a company registered in England and Wales, company number 16835419. Registered office: 32 Portway Drive, Croughton, Brackley, England, NN13 5NA.

The portal runs on Laurus Operating System (LOS) technology. MM Recruitment Ltd is an independent business and is not owned by Laurus Group.

3. Your account

  • You must be 18 or over and, where required, have the right to work in the UK.
  • You need to verify your email address before you can sign in.
  • Keep your password secure and do not share your account. Tell us straight away at mandy@mmrecruitmentltd.co.uk if you think someone else has used it.
  • One account per person.

4. What the portal lets you do

  • maintain your profile: contact details, headline, postcode area, skills, work experience, availability and job preferences;
  • upload, replace and download your CV (PDF or Word, up to 5MB);
  • view open roles and track your applications;
  • receive updates and messages from MM Recruitment, and reply to them;
  • see which employers your profile or CV has been shared with, and for which vacancy;
  • list organisations you do not want your profile shared with;
  • set your marketing preference; and
  • export your data and permanently delete your account.

5. Information you provide

Everything you add must be accurate, honest and up to date. Do not upload anything you are not entitled to share, or anything unlawful, offensive or containing malicious code.

MM Recruitment will obtain your agreement before submitting your profile and CV to a specific employer. We never share your profile with an organisation on your exclusion list.

6. No guarantee of work

We will use reasonable efforts to match you with suitable opportunities, but we cannot guarantee interviews, offers or employment. Decisions to interview or hire are made by employers.

7. Fees

MM Recruitment does not charge candidates for finding them work.

8. Acceptable use

Do not misuse the portal, including trying to access another person’s account or data, interfering with its security, or using automated tools to extract information from it.

9. Availability and changes

We aim to keep the portal available but do not guarantee uninterrupted access, and we may update or change its features. If we make material changes to these terms we will give you at least 30 days’ notice where reasonably practicable, unless a change is needed sooner because of law, regulation or security.

10. Closing your account

You can permanently delete your account at any time from Account and privacy in the portal. We may suspend or close an account that breaks these terms or is used unlawfully. Our Privacy Policy explains what happens to your information.
