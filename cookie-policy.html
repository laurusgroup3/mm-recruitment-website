Cookie & Browser Storage Policy

Draft for review - not yet finalised This page reflects the site as currently built, in Phase 1: a static marketing website with no analytics, advertising or tracking cookies configured. If analytics or any other cookie-setting technology is added later, this page must be updated to match before it goes live.

Last updated: TBC

1. What this page covers

This policy explains how MM Recruitment Ltd's website uses cookies and similar browser storage technologies.

2. Cookies we currently use

As built, this website does not set any analytics, advertising or tracking cookies. We do not currently use a cookie-consent banner because we do not currently set any non-essential cookies.

3. Essential technical storage

Our hosting provider (Cloudflare) may use essential technical mechanisms necessary to deliver the website securely and reliably. These are not used to track you across other websites.

4. Future changes

If we introduce analytics, marketing cookies, or a candidate/employer portal that uses cookies or local storage to keep you signed in, this policy will be updated to describe exactly what is used and why, and a consent mechanism will be added where required by law.

5. Contact us

Questions about this policy can be sent to TBC or via our contact page.
