Cookie & Browser Storage Policy

Last updated: 7 October 2026

1. What this page covers

This policy explains how MM Recruitment Ltd’s website (mmrecruitmentltd.co.uk) and candidate and employer portal (portal.mmrecruitmentltd.co.uk) use cookies and similar browser storage.

2. No tracking or advertising cookies

Neither the website nor the portal uses analytics, advertising or tracking cookies.

3. Portal sign-in storage

When you sign in to the portal, your sign-in session is saved in your browser’s local storage (under the name mmRecruitmentSession) so that you stay signed in as you move between pages. It is strictly necessary for the portal to work, is used only by the portal, and is removed when you sign out.

4. Fonts

The website and portal load their fonts from Google Fonts. To deliver the font files, Google receives your IP address and basic browser information. Google Fonts does not set cookies on our sites.

5. Essential technical mechanisms

Our hosting provider, Cloudflare, may use essential technical mechanisms to deliver the website and portal securely and reliably. These are not used to track you across other websites.

6. Consent

Because we only use storage that is strictly necessary, we do not show a cookie-consent banner. If we ever add non-essential cookies or similar technologies, we will update this policy and ask for your consent first where the law requires it.

7. Contact us

Questions about this policy can be sent to mandy@mmrecruitmentltd.co.uk or via our contact page.
